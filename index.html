Founder & head blender

Our story

Hi, I'm Courtney.

GLOW started with a simple, slightly stubborn idea: that feeling good should be easy, delicious and genuinely affordable — even in a small Lincolnshire town.

I was tired of "healthy" meaning beige, boring or a two-hour drive away, so I built the thing I wanted to order myself. Everything on the menu is blended fresh by hand from our kitchen at the Kesteven Business Centre in Sleaford — real fruit, proper superfoods, no refined sugar and no shortcuts — then sealed cold and sent straight to your door.

I obsess over the little things, from the thickness of the açaí to the swirl on top, because the details are what make it GLOW. Whether you're fuelling a workout, beating the 3pm slump or just want something that tastes like a treat but loves you back — I've got you.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is GLOW Sleaford?

On the ground floor of Kesteven Business Centre, Kesteven Street, Sleaford, Lincolnshire NG34 7DT — the old police station, a short walk from the town centre and the railway station. Walk in, or call 01529 707123.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver cold across Sleaford and the surrounding villages — £3 within 2 miles, collection free — and you can also order on Uber Eats. WhatsApp or call 01529 707123 to order direct.

What are your opening hours?

8am to 4pm, seven days a week. If you're travelling from further afield, give us a ring first on 01529 707123.

Do you have vegan and vegetarian options?

Plenty. Every juice and smoothie is fruit-first, several shakes use plant protein, bowls can be built vegan, and the salads and sandwiches include vegetarian options — look for the Vegan and Veggie tags on the menu.

How much protein is in a shake?

Every protein shake carries 25g of protein; the Gains range steps up to 30g protein with a full 5g of creatine. You can add a Creatine 5g boost (£1) or Protein 15g boost (£1.50) to any bowl, shake or latte.

What about allergens?

Nuts, milk, gluten, egg, fish, soya and sesame all appear on the menu, so tell us about any allergy when you order and we'll point you to the right thing.
